# Synplant 2 Genopatch: real-world results and limits (2023 to 2026)

Method note: this session had web search only, with no page-fetch tool. Every finding below comes from search-result extracts of the cited pages, not from full reads. Exact wording and context could not be checked, so treat claims as close paraphrases. No Reddit threads came up in any search (tried several times), so user evidence comes from KVR, Gearspace, VI-Control, Dogs on Acid and the Sonic Charge forum instead.

## What do professional reviews say about Genopatch's accuracy and limits?

### Takeaway
Reviewers call Genopatch the standout, genuinely new feature. They agree it gets very close on synth-made, short, simple sources (plucks, basses, percussion) and drifts on acoustic, complex or long sources. Most of them frame it as a generator of related variations, not a cloning tool. None of them measured accuracy.

### Cited Findings
- MusicRadar (4.5 stars) calls Genopatch an excellent, genuinely unique and effective use of AI. The main criticism is the dated UI and preset browser, not accuracy. — [MusicRadar](https://www.musicradar.com/reviews/sonic-charge-synplant-2-review)
- MusicRadar / Future Music (Si Truss): with synth-made sources, Synplant often gets "close to bang-on" after it has had time to work. Non-synthetic sources come out less accurate because it is limited to its own engine. — [MusicRadar](https://www.musicradar.com/reviews/sonic-charge-synplant-2-review); [Yahoo syndication](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/sonic-charge-synplant-2-review-091835233.html)
- Sound On Sound: Genopatch uses up to about two seconds of an imported sample as its reference. It works best on short sources such as plucks, basses and percussion. A realistic, fully playable instrument from a two-second sample of one guitar note is an unrealistic expectation. Matching is CPU-heavy. — [Sound On Sound](https://www.soundonsound.com/reviews/sonic-charge-synplant-2)
- MusicTech (8/10) praises the unique, inspiring patch creation and high-quality output. Its listed cons include Genopatch converting audio to mono before analysis, a basic preset system with no tagging, and fiddly DNA editing. It suggests treating results as a source of variations rather than exact matches, with uneven accuracy across drums, bass and organic instruments. — [MusicTech](https://musictech.com/reviews/plug-ins/sonic-charges-synplant-2-review/)
- Synth and Software: only a short segment of a long sample (roughly 1 to 2 s) is analysed. The machine analysis works "quite nicely with simple sounds." — [Synth and Software](https://synthandsoftware.com/2023/11/sonic-charge-synplant-2-the-synth-and-software-review/)
- Yahoo / Future coverage at launch: no sampling is involved. The synth evolves patches that get ever closer to the file, but a perfect match does not always arrive. — [Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/synplant-back-version-2-uses-104913959.html)
- Synthtopia describes four strands (branches) that grow into patches matching the audio more and more closely. — [Synthtopia](https://www.synthtopia.com/content/2023/10/04/synplant-2-lets-you-create-synth-patches-from-samples-using-artificial-intelligence/)
- Sound-Freqs review: the palette runs from organic pads to aggressive digital textures. It singles out a bell-like patch whose velocity response it liked, but notes the synthesis itself is fairly simple. — [Sound-Freqs](https://sound-freqs.com/reviews/synplant-2-review-generative-sound-design-with-ai/)
- Psychedelic Island: Genopatch is versatile with vocals. Removing modulations turns vocal samples into pads or atmospheres, which is reinterpretation rather than replication. — [Psychedelic Island](https://psychedelicisland.com/reviews/synplant-2-ai-synthesizer/)
- Not found: Attack Magazine, Gearnews and Bedroom Producers Blog reviews did not appear in targeted searches. CDM published a hands-on and a long developer interview instead. — [CDM hands-on](https://cdm.link/synplant-2-hands-on/); [CDM road to Synplant 2](https://cdm.link/the-road-to-synplant-2/)

### Inferences
- The consensus maps directly onto the engine's expressive range. Sources that could have come from a 2-osc subtractive/FM synth get matched; acoustic, layered, heavily processed or time-varying sources get an impression.
- The roughly 2 s window plus mono analysis means reviewers effectively judge a single note's static timbre and envelope. Stereo width, long evolutions and rhythmic content are outside what it matches.

### Gaps
- No review gives numbers (how many tries, similarity scores, or a per-category hit rate).
- Attack, Gearnews and BPB coverage was not found. I could not confirm whether it exists.

## What do users on forums report: what works, what fails, and tips?

### Takeaway
User reports split sharply. Some call it incredible on bass, drums and FX, others "disgraceful" on the same categories. The consistent pattern is that simple, synth-made, single-note, dry, short sources succeed. Complex modulation (neuro or rhythmic basses), claps, long soundscapes, chords and acoustic recordings fail or drift. Tips centre on short clean sources and on growing from an existing patch.

### Cited Findings
- Dogs on Acid (drum and bass producers) report contradictory results. One user found it did an incredible job on bass, kicks, hats, snares and FX. Another found it very poor at basses and drum one-shots. A demo tester reported big problems with even slightly complex sounds, let alone neuro basses. Another said it matched a complex modulated bass of his own pretty well. — [Dogs on Acid p1](https://www.dogsonacid.com/threads/synplant-2-generate-patches-from-any-sample.822242/); [p2](https://www.dogsonacid.com/threads/synplant-2-generate-patches-from-any-sample.822242/page-2); [p3](https://www.dogsonacid.com/threads/synplant-2-generate-patches-from-any-sample.822242/page-3)
- Drums: trap snares and kicks fed in produced a buzzy bell sound on one seed. Tweaking modulation then gave a bitcrushed mallet or buzzsaw bass, so an unexpected but usable result. Claps were reported as tricky. A Gearspace poster says it does well with pitched percussion. — [Dogs on Acid](https://www.dogsonacid.com/threads/synplant-2-generate-patches-from-any-sample.822242/); [Gearspace](https://gearspace.com/board/new-product-alert-2-older-threads/1414807-synplant-2-here-2.html)
- KVR: one user found it underwhelming and not playable across the keyboard without tweaking. Another found results usable but admitted he could have got closer with extra work, having applied no tweaks. — [KVR p22](https://www.kvraudio.com/forum/viewtopic.php?t=595090&start=315)
- A forum tester says it really struggles with sounds that have rhythmic complexity. — [KVR p22](https://www.kvraudio.com/forum/viewtopic.php?t=595090&start=315)
- VI-Control: a user working mainly on orchestral recordings says some outputs come "stunningly close." Another reports the Correct Tuning option sometimes distorts results. Others found the envelope and LFO hard to work with. — [VI-Control 144529](https://vi-control.net/community/threads/synplant-2.144529/); [VI-Control 141295 p2](https://vi-control.net/community/threads/synplant-2.141295/page-2)
- KVR: samples made with subtractive synthesis plus a bit of FM match best, because that is what the engine can produce. — [KVR p27](https://www.kvraudio.com/forum/viewtopic.php?t=595090&start=390)
- Sonic Charge forum: simple inputs give near-exact copies, complex inputs give something different. — [Sonic Charge forum, sample pack legality](https://soniccharge.com/forum/topic/2641-synplant-sample-pack-legality)
- Sonic Charge forum feature request on systematic failure modes: one-shot samples can produce looping results, and dry samples often pick up reverb. The user asks for manual guidance to steer the search. — [Sonic Charge forum, manual guidance](https://soniccharge.com/forum/topic/2583-feature-request-some-manual-guidance-for-genopatch)
- Length: users find the roughly 2 s limit annoying and also ask for live input. One says the limit can be raised in Genopatch's configuration, but longer material may not work well. — [Sonic Charge forum, sample length](https://soniccharge.com/forum/topic/2481-sample-length-limitation-is-so-annoying-and-what-about-live-input-recording); [KVR p33](https://www.kvraudio.com/forum/viewtopic.php?t=595090&start=480)
- Conflict: the official 2.0.2 manual mentions truncation of samples longer than 10 minutes. This seems to be the import limit, while the analysis window is about 2 s per reviewers. — [Synplant User Guide 2.0.2](https://soniccharge.com/public/Synplant%20User%20Guide.pdf)
- Tip, evolve from current patch: load a preset, load a reference, and plant the current patch so it grows toward the reference. Auto-preview is off in this mode. — [Sonic Charge forum, evolve](https://soniccharge.com/forum/topic/2501-genopatch-evolve-from-current-patch)
- Tip: once a good patch is found, autosample it into a static instrument. The patch alone is shareable, since samples are used only during generation. — [Dogs on Acid](https://www.dogsonacid.com/threads/synplant-2-generate-patches-from-any-sample.822242/)
- A user asks whether candidates are saved temporarily, which suggests people browse many candidates and lose good ones. — [Sonic Charge forum, candidates](https://soniccharge.com/forum/topic/2509-are-genopatch-candidates-saved-somewhere-temporarily)
- Speed: long, complex soundscape samples can take a few minutes before the 4 DNA branches have evolved. — [KVR p22](https://www.kvraudio.com/forum/viewtopic.php?t=595090&start=315)
- CPU: Genopatch pins the CPU at 100% while searching, and some users reported PC crashes. — [Sonic Charge forum, 100% CPU](https://soniccharge.com/forum/topic/2689-why-is-genopatch-using-100-cpu); [Sonic Charge forum, crash](https://soniccharge.com/forum/topic/2485-synplant-2-genopatch-causing-pc-crash)
- Praise: the mismatch between input and output leads to inspiring results, and users also like the built-in reverb colour on Genopatches. — [KVR p22](https://www.kvraudio.com/forum/viewtopic.php?t=595090&start=315); [Gearspace](https://gearspace.com/board/new-product-alert-2-older-threads/1414807-synplant-2-here-2.html)

### Inferences
- The contradictory bass and drum reports probably reflect source type. Simple 808s and subtractive basses work; layered, distorted, resampled neuro or dubstep basses do not, because the engine has no wavetable, layering-chain or heavy multiband processing.
- "Playable across the keyboard" is a separate failure from "sounds like the reference at one pitch". A match is fitted at one note, so key tracking is not checked.
- The dry-in, reverb-out complaint suggests the loss or search tends to fill in decay with the reverb. For Zero Engine this argues for scoring with and without FX, which Reference Match already does.
- How many tries it takes: users typically pick from the 4 branches of one growth run, sometimes after several reruns and tweaks. No one gave a count.

### Gaps
- No Reddit evidence surfaced in this session.
- No reports specific to dubstep wobbles, supersaws, synthwave leads or heavily processed EDM. Only the neuro bass complaint is close.
- No quantified tries-per-success or time-per-run figures beyond "a few minutes" for long soundscapes.
- No dedicated reports on chords or loops failing. Inferred from the 2 s mono, single-patch design (see below).

## Developer acknowledgements of limitations (Magnus Lidström)

### Takeaway
Lidström describes Genopatch as networks trained on the synth's own semi-random output to map sound to parameters, combined with a CPU-only search that renders hundreds or thousands of sounds per second. He acknowledges single-note patches and heavy CPU use. Most limits are stated implicitly ("give it anything and it will give its best shot") rather than as a failure list.

### Cited Findings
- The networks were trained on semi-random sounds generated by Synplant itself so they learn to go from output sound back to parameters. Everything runs locally on the CPU. — [Sonic Charge forum, how was ML trained](https://soniccharge.com/forum/topic/2589-how-was-synplant-2s-ml-trained); [CDM road to Synplant 2](https://cdm.link/the-road-to-synplant-2/)
- The search runs the engine at high speed, rendering hundreds or thousands of sounds per second, which is why it uses 100% CPU. Playing the resulting patches is light. — [Sonic Charge forum, 100% CPU](https://soniccharge.com/forum/topic/2689-why-is-genopatch-using-100-cpu)
- Genopatch 1.0 generates single-note patches. It can recreate simple chords using the two oscillators plus FM. You can throw anything at it and it will try its best. — [CDM (Genopatch update preview)](https://cdm.link/2023/03/synplant-2-genopatch-update/); [Synth Anatomy](https://synthanatomy.com/2023/10/sonic-charge-synplant-2-popular-synth-plugin-gets-a-genetic-sound-design-boost.html)
- Caution: the "over 200 tags, trained on almost 60,000 patches, holes in genres and exotic instruments" quote refers to PhenoType (2026 text-to-patch), not Genopatch. — [CDM PhenoType](https://cdm.link/synplant-phenotype/)

### Inferences
- The design (an amortised network guess followed by an evolutionary search on rendered audio, with 4 parallel branches) is close to Zero Engine's own approach of preset ranking, parallel refinement and finalists. The main differences are a learned initial guess and a much smaller synth.

### Gaps
- I could not read the full CDM interview text, so the details of the loss function and features (spectral or envelope, how pitch is handled) are unconfirmed.

## How people use it in practice: exact copy or starting point?

### Takeaway
Most reviewers and users treat it as an inspiration engine: a seed to mutate, edit and resample. A minority (synth-source users, an orchestral user) report close copies.

### Cited Findings
- MusicTech advises using it for variations, not exact matches. — [MusicTech](https://musictech.com/reviews/plug-ins/sonic-charges-synplant-2-review/)
- KVR: Genopatch is meant to approximate sounds roughly, not reproduce them faithfully. A user in the same thread says Icarus 2 gives better resynthesis results. — [KVR Genopatch alternative](https://www.kvraudio.com/forum/viewtopic.php?t=604148)
- Typical workflows: grow from a sample, then mutate the seed; strip modulations to turn vocals into pads; autosample the favourites. — [Psychedelic Island](https://psychedelicisland.com/reviews/synplant-2-ai-synthesizer/); [Dogs on Acid](https://www.dogsonacid.com/threads/synplant-2-generate-patches-from-any-sample.822242/)
- PhenoType (June 2026, free for owners) adds text-to-patch. A review finds its sounds often basic but usable with processing, and its FX, realistic textures and vocal-like results to be suggestions only. — [Polarity](https://polarity.me/posts/polarity-music/2026-06-10-synplant-2-phenotype-review-ai-text-to-patch-synth/); [MusicRadar](https://www.musicradar.com/music-tech/is-synplant-2s-new-prompt-based-patch-generator-the-future-of-synthesis)
- No Genopatch changes in 2025 to 2026 were found. Coverage still describes the 2023 behaviour (v2.0.2). — [CDM PhenoType](https://cdm.link/synplant-phenotype/)

### Inferences
- "Inspiration, not cloning" is a fair expectation for a sample-to-patch feature on a limited synth. Zero Engine's closeness word, which tells the user how near the match is, addresses the same honesty problem.

### Gaps
- No usage survey exists. The balance between copy and inspiration use is inferred from tone.

## Comparisons with other audio-to-patch tools

### Takeaway
No rigorous side-by-side tests exist. MicroMusic (for Vital, 2024) is reported as weaker: static subtractive results with no envelopes or modulation and a single output. Icarus 2 is cited by one user as better for resynthesis. Academic work (InverSynth, the AST sound-matching paper) tests on synthetic in-domain targets and does not benchmark Synplant.

### Cited Findings
- MicroMusic generates Vital presets from audio with ML. It was briefly free, then paid. — [Synthtopia](https://www.synthtopia.com/content/2024/05/13/micromusic-ai-powered-synth-preset-generator-recreates-patches-from-audio/); [KVR MicroMusic](https://www.kvraudio.com/forum/viewtopic.php?t=606759)
- Testers of the MicroMusic alpha and beta say it does not attempt ADSR or any modulation. A bass was "missed by a mile," a complex keyboard part was not usable, and output was basically always a simple subtractive sound. It gives a single preset, so no variation choice. One tester saw some use for textures and pads. — [Dogs on Acid MicroMusic](https://www.dogsonacid.com/threads/micro-music-ai-synth-replicator-for-vital.823849/); [p2](https://www.dogsonacid.com/threads/micro-music-ai-synth-replicator-for-vital.823849/page-2); [KVR p2](https://www.kvraudio.com/forum/viewtopic.php?t=606759&start=15); [Dataconomy](https://dataconomy.com/tools/micromusic/)
- A forum user says MicroMusic and Genopatch only loosely resemble each other. — [KVR MicroMusic](https://www.kvraudio.com/forum/viewtopic.php?t=606759)
- Gearspace compares Synplant with Backbone and Emergent Drums for drum variations, which is a different job: sample manipulation and generative drum models rather than patch inference. — [Gearspace](https://gearspace.com/board/music-computers/1420593-creating-variations-drum-samples-backbone-vs-emergent-drums-vs-synplant-etc.html)
- The academic AST sound-matching paper evaluates on NI Massive with 16 parameters, using in-domain synthetic targets, and beats MLP/CNN baselines. A 2026 paper studies off-manifold robustness, meaning targets the synth cannot make, citing InverSynth I and II. Neither benchmarks Synplant. — [arXiv 2407.16643](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.16643); [arXiv 2609.29320](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.29320)
- A community survey of AI patch-generation tools exists, which is useful for finding further alternatives. — [0xdevalias gist](https://gist.github.com/0xdevalias/5a06349b376d01b2a76ad27a86b08c1b)

### Inferences
- The off-manifold problem in the academic work (real references the synth cannot reproduce) is exactly where Genopatch users report failure. In-domain tests overstate real-world accuracy, so Zero Engine's matchbench on engine-made targets should be read the same way.
- Genopatch's edge over MicroMusic, per users, is envelopes and modulation plus several candidates to choose from, which shows those matter to users.

### Gaps
- No head-to-head tests with Icarus 2, Vital MicroMusic or academic tools on the same references.
- The paid MicroMusic releases after 2024 were not evaluated in the sources found.
