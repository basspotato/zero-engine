# Synthwave / Retrowave / Darksynth: sound design targets (Bass, Lead, Chord, Pad, Pluck, FX)

Scope note: only WebSearch was available (no full-page fetch), so findings come from search-result summaries of the cited pages. Where a page was cut off, that is noted. Numbers under "Inferences" with no citation are **estimates** for engine tuning, not sourced facts.

## Oscillator source per type (and which artists exemplify each)

### Takeaway
The genre's core is Juno-style saw (+ pulse/PWM, + sub) through a 1–2 voice-per-note analog filter and BBD chorus; leads add 2–3 voice detune/unison; brass/pads lean on two detuned saws (Jupiter-8, OB) or PWM (CS-80); basses are saw/square sequenced 16ths; DX7 FM supplies bell/e-piano plucks.

### Cited Findings
- Juno-60 chorus has two buttons with slow modulation rates of 0.4 Hz (I) and 0.6 Hz (II) — [Wikipedia: Roland Juno-60](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roland_Juno-60)
- The Midnight treat the Juno-6 as the "beating heart" of their sound (Tyler Lyle interview headline) — [inkl / MusicRadar](https://www.inkl.com/news/if-the-porsche-911-is-the-platonic-ideal-of-a-sports-car-and-the-submariner-rolex-is-the-ideal-of-a-watch-the-juno-6-is-that-for-the-midnight-it-s-in-its-beating-heart-tyler-lyle-talks-essential-gear-sax-appeal-and-taking-synthwave-on-the-road)
- Tim McEwan (The Midnight) on Neon Medusa stream: u-he Repro for arpeggios, Zebra2 "HS Animated Bells" preset, u-he Diva and Serum for bass (crowdsourced, partly unconfirmed) — [Equipboard](https://equipboard.com/pros/the-midnight); McEwan says he now owns real analogue synths — [MusicRadar](https://musicradar.com/news/the-midnight-tim-mcewan-synths-plugins-interview)
- Timecop1983: FL Studio + mostly free plugins, increasingly hardware; Juno-106, Alpha Juno, D-50, TAL U-NO-62; plucked basses where the envelope drives cutoff for a guitar-like effect — [Reverb Machine: Timecop1983 Synth Sounds](https://reverbmachine.com/blog/timecop1983-synth-sounds/)
- Timecop1983 "Lovers" lead: pulse-width square mixed into the saw, PWM in LFO mode, Chorus I — [Reverb Machine](https://reverbmachine.com/blog/timecop1983-synth-sounds/)
- Timecop1983 "On The Run" lead: detuned saw + basic wavetable, LFO1 on phase, LFO2 on fine tune, flanger and Erosion, sidechained to kick — [Synth Ctrl](https://synthctrl.com/blogs/blog/timecop1983-on-the-run-breakdown)
- Kavinsky "Nightcall" main synth: saw-based, plucked envelope; recreated in TAL U-NO-LX (Juno-60 emulation) with chorus, sub oscillator off; Kavinsky likely did not use a Juno-60 originally — [Reverb Machine: How Kavinsky Created Nightcall](https://reverbmachine.com/blog/how-kavinsky-created-nightcall/)
- Nightcall lead as 3-voice unison saw arp, resonant low-pass decay, 80s chorus for width, low end cut by EQ — [Syntorial preset recipe](https://www.syntorial.com/preset-recipe/kavinsky-nightcall-lead/)
- Arturia Kavinsky Tribute: 32 presets built on Mini V, Prophet V etc. (Moog/Prophet sources for Outrun-era sounds) — [Arturia](https://www.arturia.com/store/de/kavinsky_tribute)
- Gunship used Juno-106, DSI Prophet 12, Prophet 6, Minimoog — [Rawckus](https://www.rawckus.com/5-artists-making-synthwave-today/); debut described as "bassy arpeggios" and "booming drum machines" — [Electronic Sound](https://www.electronicsound.co.uk/reviews/gunship-gunship-ingrooves/)
- Perturbator uses software emulations of vintage synths such as OB-X and CS-80, and from New Model on applies distortion across his synths — [Wikipedia: Perturbator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perturbator); [mxdwn review of New Model](https://music.mxdwn.com/2017/11/05/reviews/perturbator-new-model/)
- Carpenter Brut style built on analog synths, dark retro-futurist aesthetic; commercial "Carpenter Tones" Serum pack has 17 basses of 50 presets — [rekkerd.org](https://rekkerd.org/carpenter-tones-soundset-for-serum-by-tonepusher)
- Com Truise: detuned patches with filter envelope through chorus and rhythmic delay; has praised the Prophet 08 — [Reverb Machine: Com Truise Synth Sounds](https://reverbmachine.com/blog/com-truise-synth-sounds); Minimoog-style bass recipe: two saws same octave, one detuned "about 15%", filter env pluck, compression + saturation — [MusicTech Com Truise guide](https://musictech.com/guides/essential-guide/produce-dreamy-synths-retro-drums-like-com-truise)
- Vangelis Blade Runner brass (CS-80): PWM plus dynamic filter modulation, heavy velocity/aftertouch; recreation recipes use all-saw, low resonance, filter ~3/4 open, 12 dB/oct, heavy reverb — [Syntorial](https://www.syntorial.com/preset-recipe/vangelis-blade-runner-brass/); [Moog forum](https://forum.moogmusic.com/viewtopic.php?p=112587); [MusicTech Blade Runner](https://musictech.com/tutorials/how-to-emulate-sounds-of-blade-runner/)
- MusicTech ES2 Blade Runner recipe: two saws mixed ~60/40, osc 2 detuned "a couple of cents" — [MusicTech](https://musictech.com/tutorials/how-to-emulate-sounds-of-blade-runner/)
- Jupiter-8 pad (Roland): two saws equal level, VCO-2 fine tune ±7 — [Roland Articles](https://articles.roland.com/sound-design-jupiter-8-model-expansion-poly-presets/); Jupiter-8 has only two oscillators per voice in poly mode — [Vital forum](https://forum.vital.audio/t/another-classic-80s-sound-no-external-effects/3415)
- OB-series thickness comes largely from per-voice tuning variation (trimpots), so explicit detune is often unnecessary — [Gearspace "Let's talk OB-X"](https://gearspace.com/threads/lets-talk-ob-x.587868/)
- DX7 best known for electric piano, bell, plucked/percussive sounds (32 algorithms, sine operators) — [Loopmasters Synth Explorer DX7](https://www.loopmasters.com/products/3941-Synth-Explorer-DX7)
- Juno pad recipes: saw full + square half at 8' (Attack/Roland Cloud); 808 State-style pad: square off, saw full, HPF raised to reduce bass weight — [Attack Magazine](https://www.attackmagazine.com/?p=133239)

### Inferences
- Engine sources per type (estimates):
  - **Bass**: saw + square (or saw + sub −1 oct square), 2 voices detuned ~5–10 cents, mono; darksynth variant adds pre-filter drive.
  - **Lead**: saw + PWM pulse, 2–3 voice unison ~8–15 cents; optional mono/glide 30–80 ms for "screaming" darksynth/Lazerhawk leads.
  - **Chord/brass**: 2 saws detuned ~±5–7 cents (Jupiter/OB) or PWM pulse (CS-80 brass), per-voice random pitch drift ±2–4 cents ("OB trimpot" spread).
  - **Pad**: single saw (+ optional PWM 0.3–1 Hz) with Juno chorus doing the width; slow amp envelope.
  - **Pluck**: either Juno saw with fast filter decay, or 2-op FM bell/e-piano (ratio 1:1 e-piano, ~1:3.5 or non-integer bell).
  - **FX**: white noise + saw/sine with pitch/filter sweeps, through long reverb/delay.
- Lazerhawk and FM-84 have no sourced gear data here; FM-84's sound is commonly associated with Juno-style chorused synths and gated drums, but unverified.

### Gaps
- No reliable source found for FM-84 or Lazerhawk gear/patches, nor Gunship using Jupiter/OB specifically.
- No sourced Juno chorus depth/delay-time values (only rates). Third-party clones disagree: SoundForce Chorus 6 I+II mode "1 Hz, 8% depth" ([ModularGrid](https://modulargrid.net/e/modules/view/43993)); TC June-60 lists slow 0.5/0.83 Hz, fast 1.3/2.2 Hz ([manual PDF](https://www.allfordj.ru/upload/iblock/a19/wmxxexutu0d00gcp3g30kemcjy0zqd4r/rukovodstvo_polzovatelya_tc_electronic_june_60_v2.pdf)) — conflicts with Wikipedia's 0.4/0.6 Hz.

## Filter and envelope shapes (ms)

### Takeaway
Hard numbers exist for Juno-106 ranges and a few recipes: brass/pad attacks 250–400 ms, bass/pluck 0 ms attack with 200–600 ms decay and 0 sustain; filters mostly low resonance, 24 dB Juno-style LPF (12 dB for CS-80 brass), with leads/plucks using a strong filter envelope.

### Cited Findings
- Juno-106 spec: envelope attack 1.5 ms–3 s; decay and release 1.5 ms–12 s; LFO 0.1–30 Hz; LFO delay 0–3 s — [Roland support: Juno-106 specs](https://support.roland.com/hc/en-us/articles/201966419-Juno-106-Technical-Specifications)
- Blade Runner brass recreation: mono voice, amp attack 250 ms, decay 1900 ms (approximate) — [Syntorial](https://www.syntorial.com/preset-recipe/vangelis-blade-runner-brass/); 2-pole 12 dB filters, low resonance — [Moog forum](https://forum.moogmusic.com/viewtopic.php?p=112587)
- Jupiter-8 pad: attack ~400, release ~500–550 (Roland model units), VCF freq lowered to 600–700, env mod ~70 on Env-2 — [Roland Articles](https://articles.roland.com/sound-design-jupiter-8-model-expansion-poly-presets/)
- 16th-note synth bass (Logic Retro Synth): saw + square, voice detune, filter env ~0 ms attack, 590 ms decay, 0 sustain, 270 ms release; accents via velocity → filter env depth plus octave shift — [MusicTech](https://musictech.com/2015/09/logic-synth-bass-sbs/)
- Serum synthwave bass: saw 1–2 oct down, 8 unison voices, amp decay 200–400 ms, sustain 0, release ~100 ms — [Slooply](https://slooply.com/blog/how-to-make-a-synthwave-bass-in-serum-2-free-presets-inside/)
- Timecop1983 "Eyes Closed" lead (TAL U-NO-LX sliders 0–10): cutoff 3, resonance 2, env amount 5, ADSR 2/5/6/7, both choruses on; "Lovers" lead ADSR 2/8/5/6 with Chorus I; pads rely on long decay/release so notes blend — [Reverb Machine](https://reverbmachine.com/blog/timecop1983-synth-sounds/)
- Nightcall lead: resonant LP with decay envelope — [Syntorial](https://www.syntorial.com/preset-recipe/kavinsky-nightcall-lead/)
- 808 State-style Juno pad: cutoff slightly lowered, touch of resonance, envelope left flat (little volume movement) — [Attack Magazine](https://www.attackmagazine.com/?p=133239)

### Inferences
- Estimates for engine profiles (converting slider positions via Juno-106 ranges, roughly exponential):
  - **Bass**: A 0–3 ms, filter D 150–350 ms (16ths at 100–118 BPM are 127–150 ms, so decay ≈ 1–2 steps), S 0–20%, R 50–150 ms; LPF 24 dB, cutoff ~300–600 Hz + env, res 10–25%.
  - **Lead**: A 5–20 ms, D 300–800 ms, S 50–70%, R 300–600 ms; cutoff mid (~1.5–3 kHz), res 15–25%, env amount moderate.
  - **Chord/brass**: A 150–400 ms on filter and amp (CS-80 brass "swell"), D 1–2 s, S 60–80%, R 400–800 ms; 12 dB LPF, low res.
  - **Pad**: A 400–1500 ms, S 80–100%, R 1.5–4 s; cutoff ~1–2.5 kHz, HPF ~150–250 Hz, res ≤15%.
  - **Pluck**: A 0–2 ms, filter D 150–400 ms, amp D 400–900 ms, S 0, R 200–500 ms.
  - **FX**: riser filter sweep over 2–8 bars (opening LPF), impact/zap: pitch env from +24–36 st down over 100–400 ms.

### Gaps
- No published millisecond values for The Midnight, FM-84, Perturbator or Carpenter Brut patches; Juno-slider-to-ms conversion is not documented.

## Modulation, chorus, delay and reverb habits

### Takeaway
Juno BBD chorus (0.4–0.6 Hz original, stereo by antiphase modulation) is the main width and motion device; PWM via LFO; delays and big reverbs (Lexicon-style halls/plates, gated reverb on drums) give the "lush space."

### Cited Findings
- Juno chorus rates 0.4 Hz (I), 0.6 Hz (II) — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roland_Juno-60); Juno-style stereo chorus modulates L and R 180° out of phase — [ModularGrid SoundForce Chorus 6](https://modulargrid.net/e/modules/view/43993)
- Juno-106 PWM: LFO or manual; LFO range 0.1–30 Hz — [Roland support](https://support.roland.com/hc/en-us/articles/201966419-Juno-106-Technical-Specifications)
- Timecop1983: "lush ambient space" through delay and reverb is a key part of the sound — [Reverb Machine](https://reverbmachine.com/blog/timecop1983-synth-sounds/)
- Com Truise: chorus and rhythmic delay on detuned patches — [Reverb Machine](https://reverbmachine.com/blog/com-truise-synth-sounds)
- Blade Runner: Lexicon 224 reverb, velocity/aftertouch modulation — [synthtopia summary of Reverb Machine breakdown](https://www.synthtopia.com/?p=151199); heavy reverb — [Moog forum](https://forum.moogmusic.com/viewtopic.php?p=112587)
- Future Music 80s track: Valhalla VintageVerb send on arp, 40% wet insert on lead — [Future Music via PressReader](https://www.pressreader.com/australia/future-music-9629/20200305/282041919167588)
- The Midnight use Valhalla ÜberMod (multitap delay/echo) — [Equipboard](https://equipboard.com/pros/the-midnight)
- Gated reverb: 2–3 s decay, gate after reverb keyed from the dry snare, fast attack, fast release so the tail cuts abruptly — [Unison](https://unison.audio/?p=1377494); [MusicTech vintage drum reverb](https://musictech.com/tutorials/tips/how-to-create-authentic-vintage-drum-gated-reverb-for-synthwave-chillwave-music-styles)
- Timecop1983 lead sidechained to kick — [Synth Ctrl](https://synthctrl.com/blogs/blog/timecop1983-on-the-run-breakdown)
- Vibrato/expression is central to CS-80 brass; commenters stress playing technique (aftertouch) — [Moog forum](https://forum.moogmusic.com/viewtopic.php?p=112587)

### Inferences
- Estimates: chorus I = 0.5 Hz, II = 0.8 Hz, I+II ~1 Hz shallower; BBD delay ~2–6 ms, depth ±1–3 ms. PWM LFO 0.3–1.5 Hz on pads, 2–5 Hz on leads. Lead vibrato 4.5–6 Hz, depth 10–25 cents, delayed 200–500 ms. Delay: dotted 1/8 (at 100 BPM = 450 ms; at 118 BPM ≈ 381 ms) with 25–40% feedback, low-passed ~3–5 kHz, wet 15–30% on leads/plucks. Reverb: hall/plate 2.5–4 s on pads/leads (wet 20–40%), shorter 1–1.5 s on plucks; gated reverb is a drum treatment and an FX-snare one-shot option. Sidechain/pump 3–6 dB on pads/chords at quarter-note.

### Gaps
- No primary source for "dotted-1/8 delay is the synthwave default"; widely repeated in tutorials but not sourced here.
- No measured Juno chorus delay times.

## Saturation and compression

### Takeaway
Synthwave (The Midnight, Timecop1983) is clean-to-warm; darksynth (Perturbator, Carpenter Brut) uses obvious distortion on synths and basses. Compression is gentle (LA-2A/1176-style) on melodic parts.

### Cited Findings
- Perturbator: distortion permeates synths from New Model (2017) — [mxdwn](https://music.mxdwn.com/2017/11/05/reviews/perturbator-new-model/)
- Darksynth preset packs centre on "aggressive distorted basses" (Darksynth Terror for Pigments: 21 basses of 68) — [ADSR](https://www.adsrsounds.com/product/presets/patchamker-darksynth-terror-for-arturia-pigments-presets/)
- Com Truise-style bass: compression + saturation for "growl" — [MusicTech](https://musictech.com/guides/essential-guide/produce-dreamy-synths-retro-drums-like-com-truise)
- Tim McEwan: FabFilter Pro-Q 3 workhorse, VC76/VC2A gentle control, Boz +10dB for fast attack — [MusicRadar](https://musicradar.com/news/the-midnight-tim-mcewan-synths-plugins-interview)
- Timecop1983 "On The Run" uses Ableton Erosion (digital grit) — [Synth Ctrl](https://synthctrl.com/blogs/blog/timecop1983-on-the-run-breakdown)

### Inferences
- Estimates: synthwave profiles = soft tape-style saturation, 1–3 dB of added harmonics, gentle bus compression (2:1, 2–4 dB GR). Darksynth bass/lead = drive 6–15 dB into asymmetric clipper/tube, then LPF ~5–8 kHz to tame fizz; respects the engine's clean-sub/dirty-mids split (sub below ~100 Hz kept clean).

### Gaps
- No sourced tape-emulation settings or Com Truise "wobble" method (likely modulation/delay, unconfirmed).

## Stereo width and mono low end

### Takeaway
Width is chorus-driven (antiphase BBD), not wide unison; bass is mono and leads/plucks often have low end cut.

### Cited Findings
- Juno chorus stereo via 180° out-of-phase L/R modulation — [ModularGrid](https://modulargrid.net/e/modules/view/43993)
- Nightcall lead: chorus for width, aggressive low-end EQ cut — [Syntorial](https://www.syntorial.com/preset-recipe/kavinsky-nightcall-lead/)
- Timecop1983 lead: both chorus modes on "for a really wide sound" — [Reverb Machine](https://reverbmachine.com/blog/timecop1983-synth-sounds/)
- Juno pad: HPF raised to reduce bass weight — [Attack Magazine](https://www.attackmagazine.com/?p=133239)

### Inferences
- Estimates: Bass mono below ~120–150 Hz, chorus off or mono-compatible; Lead moderately wide (side share ~20–35%), correlation >0.5; Pad/Chord widest (side 35–50%, correlation 0.2–0.5); Pluck 25–40%; FX can be fully wide. HPF leads/chords/plucks at ~150–250 Hz, pads ~120–200 Hz.

### Gaps
- No measured width/correlation data for genre references; validate with `ZeroAnalyze analyze` on owner references.

## Brightness, loudness, transient character

### Takeaway
No synthwave-specific loudness data found; general electronic masters sit −9 to −14 LUFS. Character is darker and softer-transient than EDM: filtered saws, slow pad/brass attacks, plucks being the main transient sources.

### Cited Findings
- Final masters −10 to −14 LUFS integrated typical — [Waves](https://www.waves.com/how-many-lufs-should-my-master-be); electronic music often −11 or louder — [Plugin Alliance](https://plugin-alliance.com/en/blog/blogpost/items/mixing_and_mastering_tips_how_loud_should_your_master_be.html); "most modern music −9 LUFS or louder" (anecdotal) — [KVR](https://www.kvraudio.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=8977753)
- Leads/pads low-pass filtered, low resonance (Jupiter cutoff lowered "to smooth out"; Timecop1983 cutoff 3/10) — [Roland Articles](https://articles.roland.com/sound-design-jupiter-8-model-expansion-poly-presets/); [Reverb Machine](https://reverbmachine.com/blog/timecop1983-synth-sounds/)

### Inferences
- Estimates for one-shot targets (synthwave / darksynth): Bass −10 to −12 / −8 to −10 LUFS short-term; Lead −12 to −14 / −10 to −12; Pad −16 to −18 (soft attack, PLR high); Chord −13 to −15; Pluck −13 to −15 with PLR 12–16 dB (transient); FX −14 to −18. Brightness: darker tilt than EDM, centroid estimate ~1–2 kHz for pads/chords, 2–3.5 kHz for leads/plucks; darksynth leads brighter/harsher. Transients: pad/brass no transient (attack ≥150 ms), bass/pluck sharp (≤3 ms).

### Gaps
- No measured LUFS, centroid or PLR for synthwave one-shots or tracks. Best route: measure owner's synthwave references with `ZeroAnalyze analyze`/`tune`.

## Register (MIDI note range) per type

### Takeaway
No source gives ranges directly; estimates below come from the patches (bass 1–2 oct down, 8' leads/pads).

### Cited Findings
- Bass: saw dropped 1–2 octaves; octave-jump accents in 16th lines — [Slooply](https://slooply.com/blog/how-to-make-a-synthwave-bass-in-serum-2-free-presets-inside/); [MusicTech](https://musictech.com/2015/09/logic-synth-bass-sbs/)
- Juno pad oscillators at 8' range — [Attack Magazine](https://www.attackmagazine.com/?p=133239)
- Nightcall: bass on B under G major arp (G/B voicing) — [Reverb Machine](https://reverbmachine.com/blog/how-kavinsky-created-nightcall/)

### Inferences
- Estimates (C3 = 60 here, Ableton/Yamaha would call it C3, Logic C4 differs): Bass E1–E3 (28–52), root mostly A1–F2 with octave jumps; Lead C4–C6 (60–84); Chord C3–C5 (48–72), 3–4 note voicings; Pad C3–C5 (48–72), often 4–5 notes; Pluck/arp C4–C6 (60–84); FX full range, risers root ~C3–C4.

### Gaps

## Priority gaps summary (Pad, Lead, FX)

### Takeaway
Pad and Lead have usable anchors (Juno/Jupiter/Timecop1983/CS-80 recipes); FX has almost no genre-specific source data beyond generic riser/zap construction.

### Cited Findings
- Pad anchors: Jupiter-8 attack ~400 / release ~500–550, two saws ±7 detune — [Roland](https://articles.roland.com/sound-design-jupiter-8-model-expansion-poly-presets/); Juno saw pad, chorus I, HPF up — [Attack Magazine](https://www.attackmagazine.com/?p=133239); long decay/release so notes blend — [Reverb Machine](https://reverbmachine.com/blog/timecop1983-synth-sounds/)
- Lead anchors: Timecop1983 saw + PWM + chorus — [Reverb Machine](https://reverbmachine.com/blog/timecop1983-synth-sounds/); CS-80 brass lead attack 250 ms / decay 1900 ms mono — [Syntorial](https://www.syntorial.com/preset-recipe/vangelis-blade-runner-brass/); Nightcall 3-voice unison saw — [Syntorial](https://www.syntorial.com/preset-recipe/kavinsky-nightcall-lead/)
- FX anchors: riser = white noise through LPF with cutoff opening over time — [MusicRadar synth sweep FX](https://www.musicradar.com/how-to/synth-sweep-fx); riser lengths 2/4/8/16 bars, noise sweeps, pitch risers, zaps, sci-fi FX are standard categories — [KVR Club Risers](https://www.kvraudio.com/news/biome-digital-release-club-risers-sample-pack---200-sweeps-uplifters-and-pitch-risers-26359); [Reason Studios Club Risers](https://reasonstudios.com/shop/product/club-risers/)

### Inferences
- Synthwave FX (estimates): (1) noise sweep riser, 4 bars, LPF 200 Hz→10 kHz, chorus + 3 s reverb; (2) "laser/zap": saw or square, pitch env down 2–3 octaves in 150–300 ms, dotted-1/8 delay; (3) reverse-swell of a pad chord; (4) gated-reverb snare/impact one-shot (2–3 s reverb gated to ~250–400 ms); (5) darksynth downlifter with heavy distortion.

### Gaps
- No artist interviews describing FX design (Perturbator/Carpenter Brut cinematic FX especially). Lazerhawk, FM-84 unresearched due to lack of sources.
