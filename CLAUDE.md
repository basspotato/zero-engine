# Zero Engine

A VST3 instrument that produces finished, release-ready electronic sounds (EDM, dubstep, techno, tech-house, trap, synthwave, plus DnB, pop, house, trance, future bass, hardstyle) out of the box. Personal studio tool, not for sale. UI: genre + type selectors, six macro knobs (Weight, Grit, Punch, Width, Motion, Air), a display (live wavetable / waveform / spectrum; the wavetable view follows the voices critically damped, lights its frames on a smooth bell and leaves a fading smoke trail of the playing frame, drawn at 60 Hz only while something moves; `ZeroAnalyze wtstrip <png>` previews a sweep), a SEQUENCER button (opens the ARP page) with ARP ON/OFF and LATCH ON/OFF beside it, a 303 row in Macros (Cutoff, Reso, Env Mod, Decay, Accent, a 303 MODE switch and SAW/SQUARE: tweaks on top of the loaded sound, kept as you generate or load sounds (undo and redo take a 303 move back; a project keeps them); a thin live waveform under its knobs shows 303 mode is on), Pan (sticks at centre) and Output knobs, an output meter, a Generate tesseract with history and favourites (MUTATE / MOOD / GENRE under it, and a heart and thumbs-down that teach Generate your taste), and an Advanced page with every parameter.

Background and decisions: `reports/AI finished sound VST engine.md` (original research), `reports/Artist sound design targets.md` (per genre/type targets the profiles are built from), `Build notes.md` (what the owner wants to use), `research_notes/` (sources).

## Build (Windows)

```bash
cmake -S . -B build -G "Visual Studio 18 2026" -A x64
cmake --build build --config Release --target ZeroEngine_VST3 ZeroEngine_Standalone
```

JUCE 8 is fetched by CMake. Outputs land in `build/ZeroEngine_artefacts/Release/` (`VST3/Zero Engine.vst3`, `Standalone/Zero Engine.exe`). A running standalone locks the `.exe`, so close it before rebuilding. `dist/` holds a prebuilt VST3 for testing on other machines; GitHub Actions (`.github/workflows/build.yml`) also builds it on every push.

## Analysis tool (offline, not shipped)

`tools/analyze/` builds `ZeroAnalyze.exe` (target `ZeroAnalyze`, output in `build/ZeroAnalyze_artefacts/Release/`). It measures loudness (BS.1770 LUFS, peak-to-loudness ratio), brightness (centroid, % above 4 kHz), bass (% below 150/60 Hz) and width (side share, correlation).

- `ZeroAnalyze analyze <folder> [out.csv]`: measure any folder of audio.
- `ZeroAnalyze render [outDir]`: render all 72 profiles to WAV and measure them.
- `ZeroAnalyze compare <refFolder> [outDir]`: measure references laid out as `<Genre>/<Type>/*.wav`, render the matching profiles, print ref vs engine.
- `ZeroAnalyze tune <refFolder> [--write] [--tone]`: for each `<Genre>/<Type>` group with 3+ references, adjust level trim, OTT depth, tilt, Weight and stereo width until the render matches the references; `--write` saves to `Source/ProfileTuning.h`. `--tone` moves tilt only (Â±2 dB), for dry references such as the owner's Jupiter-8 recordings. `tunetest` checks the tuner. Freesound references proved too mixed to write wholesale; review every dry run with the owner.
- `ZeroAnalyze test`: every profile at several rates and block sizes, plus 4 Generate results per profile, Generate's arp roll and four Reference Match cases (a plucked lead, a 5 Hz filter wobble, a dark pad, a pluck loop; the wobble case is an expected failure, reported but not counted); fails on non-finite output, clipping, tails that don't die, allocations in `processBlock`, state or favourites that don't round-trip, Generate history, or a mod wheel that does nothing. Takes about 5 minutes. `bench`: CPU with every voice (24) held.
- `ZeroAnalyze gencheck`: Generate's time per click and how many of its 24 candidates pass the check, per profile. `gendebug2 <Genre> <Type>`: every candidate's scores for two clicks. `genab [dir] [pairs]`: a blind A/B pack (plain variation vs Generate's pick, x/y in random order, key.csv) plus any-genre renders. `tastesim`: a simulated listener checks the taste model learns (temporary folder only). `library traits [source=] [--write]`: SoundTraits of library sounds per type; `--write` saves Generate's targets to `Source/GenerateTargets.h` (measurement only).
- `ZeroAnalyze gendebug <Genre> <Type> <seed> [id=value...]`: one Generate result's key settings and its level and voice count each second through a note and its tail; overrides help find what holds a sound up. `segments <wav> [s]`: loudness and brightness per slice.
- `ZeroAnalyze match <wav> <Genre> <Type> [-v]` or `match <folder> [-v]` (laid out `<Genre>/<Type>/*.wav`): runs Reference Match as the plugin does (it searches every genre/type; the one given is only where the plugin "is") and prints the closeness word and what it chose. `-v`: the reference's transcription, the starting-preset ranking, each worker's progress, the finalists and the loss term by term, and the result again with OTT, reverb/delay or the spectral stage off. `notes <wav>`: just the transcription.
- `ZeroAnalyze matchbench [refFolder] [budget=N] [cmaes=1] [light=0] [w=mel,mfcc,centroid,loudness]`: scores Reference Match on 12 engine-made targets (notes and arp loops): the match replays the target's notes and is judged by log-mel dB difference and MFCC timbre. Writes targets and matches to `%TEMP%/zero_matchbench`; with refFolder, matches the owner's references there and writes what it found to `analysis/match_v2`. Use it to judge any change to SoundPrint or ReferenceMatch.
- `ZeroAnalyze sweep`: every genre/type's Generate picks against the designed sound (brightness, width, tables), to find off-style sections.
- `ZeroAnalyze clickcheck [arp]`: clicks in Generate's picks (3 per profile, two notes each, or a held chord through the arp; a jump 4x anything within 20 ms). `picks <Genre> <Type>`: the designed sound and 8 picks with table, filter and brightness. `tastereport`: what the owner's taste models have learned (read only). `clickhunt [threshold] [dry]`: stricter, played like a person (staccato, legato, chord changes, fast 1/16s), second-difference spikes, worst 12 to analysis/clicks; `peek <wav> <s> [n]`: the samples around a moment. Notes continuing a sounding voice ease level, velocity's cutoff and pitch (2.5 ms), round the amp envelope's corners and rise the mod envelope over 4 ms at least (0 of 144 picks over 3x).
- `ZeroAnalyze crushcheck`: Generate picks (two per profile) and designed sounds squashed into the clipper (crest under the type's floor, as Generate's score has it). `idlecheck`: silence with no keys, and held notes letting go on a new sound.
- `ZeroAnalyze noise <Genre> <Type>|all [id=value...]`: noise between the harmonics of a held C4 (dB under them), to find stages that add hiss.
- `ZeroAnalyze harmonics <out.h> <5 wavs>`: harmonic spectra of recorded notes as a wavetable bank (made `Source/JupiterHarmonics.h`).
- `ZeroAnalyze snapshot <png> [size=WxH] [button...] [dialog]`: the editor as an image, after playing a chord; clicks buttons by label (e.g. `ADVANCED MOTION`). Send the owner a preview after GUI changes. `foldstrip <png>`: the tesseract's fold, frame by frame.
- `ZeroAnalyze screen` / `fetch`: Freesound CC0 search and download (needs the owner's `FREESOUND_CLIENT_SECRET` / `FREESOUND_CLIENT_ID`).

Output goes to `analysis/` by default (git-ignored).

## Layout

- `Source/Parameters.h`: parameter IDs and layout (APVTS)
- `Source/Profiles.*`: the 72 designed profiles (type template, genre shift, signatures, `applyResearchedGaps`), `generateProfile` for Generate; `keepInCharacter` holds limits a genre/type keeps on every Generate candidate (House and Pop basses stay round)
- `Source/SynthVoice.*`, `Source/Envelope.h`: voice DSP (wavetables, unison, filter, tempo-synced LFO); the 303 row (its own filter sweep restarted by each note but not a slide, accents at velocity 100+ with a short sweep, +5 dB and a slow-charging "wow" that climbs over back-to-back accents, deeper with resonance; skipped entirely at neutral). 303 MODE: the sound becomes a 303 - a PolyBLEP saw or square (one voice, no sub, no pitch modulation), a four-stage ladder (TPT, solved per sample, run 2x oversampled with input drive, tanh, passband falling with resonance) over the 303's own range (Cutoff 230 Hz to 2.8 kHz, Reso 0 to the edge of ringing), its envelope (3 ms on, slow fall, 12 ms release, no velocity), Legato with a 60 ms minimum slide, mono at Width 0 (Width up: a detuned twin on the right, the two filters apart, the widener on), OTT eased, spectral off, no tilt cut; Grit stays. Sweep and accent rise in ~1.5 ms and the accent level eases over 3 ms (no clicks). `ZeroAnalyze acidline [dir] [dry]`: a 16-step acid line in 303 mode (saw, square, a cutoff sweep) to WAV with a click check. `ZeroAnalyze acidcheck`: just the 303 row's test; analog-style exponential envelopes (fall stages stretched 1.9x so presets kept their length when they replaced the linear ones)
- `Source/Wavetable.*`: wavetable banks, including the Jupiter bank from `JupiterHarmonics.h`
- `Source/FinishingChain.*`: saturation, OTT, EQ, width, spectral stage, clipper, meters; Input Trim (per profile, before the chain) lets the thickest pads and chords hit the saturation softer. `ZeroAnalyze easeab`: those presets before and now; `delayab`: the delay before and after it stopped leaning left
- `Source/SpectralCompressor.*`: our own many-band (512-point STFT) up/down compressor, level-neutral, leaves < 150 Hz alone, lifts only bins within 30 dB of the loudest and never the gaps between harmonics, gains smoothed over 9 bins; adds 512 samples of latency
- `Source/MacroMap.h`: what each macro pushes per profile; the mod wheel adds to Motion
- `Source/Arpeggiator.*`: tempo-synced arp with a 16-step pattern that shapes every mode (rests, transpose, accent); mode "Sequence" plays all held keys per step. Runs on the MIDI before the synth; its settings are not part of profiles
- `Source/PluginProcessor.*`: synth, finishing chain, state, Generate, history, favourites (`%APPDATA%/Zero Engine/Favourites`). Generate: 24 variations (some bred from hearted sounds of the same genre and type) measured on 4 private engines in parallel; kept if still in the genre/type's character; scored per trait (width at half weight, body, density, richness; movement not rewarded, after the owner's A/B rounds) against the owner's sounds as a soft capped guide, plus novelty and the learned taste (capped at 1; one click in three ignores it, for variety); softmax pick, pushed twice towards a higher score, then an octave check (down and up; the next best if it falls apart). Tuning gates reject candidates out of tune, wobblier or noisier than the designed sound (pitch tracked through the note; not FX); detune capped. A neural judge (GenerateNet: settings in, predicted measurements out, trained on 100k engine renders) screens 400 drawn candidates down to the 24 rendered (20 best predicted + 4 at random): retrained 2026-10-29 on 100k renders (notes-only width and movement, the lead and bass moods, Mutate-style draws, targets from the owner's own projects): latest retrain 2026-10-30 (new moods, round basses): picks -0.16 vs -0.26 without it on the toned-down score (35 better, 19 worse), variety 2.10 vs 1.74 (genjudgeab). Any genre (mini tesseract): candidates from every genre and two-genre blends, loaded as the leading genre. Hearts and taste judgments (kept = played 6 s+, skipped = clicked past in 4 s without a note) in `%APPDATA%/Zero Engine/Hearts.xml` and `Taste.xml`; the same judgments, plus each heart beating three plain draws of its genre/type, also train a taste in the settings themselves (SettingsTaste: Bradley-Terry on the judge's 99 inputs, `TasteSettings.xml`), which helps choose both the 24 rendered and the pick, full strength from 100 judgments. `tastesim settings`: a listener who likes two wavetables got them in 19 of the last 30 clicks with it, 11 without; `tastesim [nosettings]`: the trait listener
- `Source/Moods.*`: Mood mode's 11 recipes (lush pad, airy drone, warm keys, glassy bell, dark texture, soft pluck, soaring lead, deep bass, pluck bass, anthem lead, stab chords), each loading as its own type; picked weighted so basses, leads and chords are about 73 %; built from the recipe only (no heart offspring); scored for warmth by mood (none for bells and leads), the lead moods on size, all with a loudness check
- `Source/SoundTraits.*`, `Source/GenerateTargets.h`, `Source/TasteModel.*`: what Generate measures, its per-type targets (the owner's own projects for Bass, Lead, Pad and Pluck; all 1,187 of their sounds for Chord and FX), and the taste model (Bradley-Terry on traits, Laplace uncertainty, Thompson sampling)
- `Source/GenerateNet.*`, `Source/GenerateNetWeights.h`, `tools/analyze/GenerateNetTrain.*`: Generate's neural judge (MLP 99 -> 128 -> 128 -> 12). `gennetdata [n]` renders and measures candidates as Generate makes them, `gennettrain [data] [epochs] [--write]` trains it (held-out R2 per output) and writes the weights header, `gennetscreen` checks its picks beat chance, `genjudgeab` compares Generate with and without it. `clarityaudit [x Genre Type] [full]`: clarity, pitch offset, wobble and noise of designed sounds and their variations
- `Source/MatchNet.*`, `tools/analyze/MatchNetTrain.*`: Match's neural-net scaffolding (unused; Match is frozen)
- `Source/SoundPrint.*`, `Source/ReferenceMatch.*`, `Source/Cmaes.h`: Reference Match. Fingerprint = log-mel spectrograms over the whole phrase (two resolutions), compared as a composite loss (mel + MFCC + centroid + loudness per frame + width); a reference is transcribed into notes (spectral-flux onsets, pitch from each note's arrived energy; echoes, ghost notes and held wobbles merged) and candidates replay those notes with a searched hold (gate). Search: every preset at the reference's pitch (FX only for unpitched sounds), the closest 6 refined in parallel on a low-priority pool (octave, wavetable, delay time, sub layer, wobble seed, pattern search over 23 settings), the 2 best get a second round; search renders are light (no spectral stage or oversampling), finalists re-scored at full quality (spectral also tried off). Can change genre/type. About 20 s. Our CMA-ES is optional (lost to the pattern search at this budget). No ML.
- `Source/PluginEditor.*`: UI (look and feel, display, meter, tesseract with its space screen, Advanced page: SOUND & MIX, and ARP with a pattern display after Magic Touch's Spectral Cloud: a ghost spectrum and glowing note bars (orange, yellow on accents) that fade into smoke; `arpstrip <png>` previews it); laid out at 960 x 760 and scaled with the window. SOUND & MIX rows tinted by stage (yellow, orange, magenta, blue, top to bottom) over a faint mixing-desk photo. `Resources/Space/`: the faint nebula behind the Generate screen, the Jupiter and Mars photos and `mixer.jpg` (the owner's; `unused/` holds earlier ones)
- `Resources/Fonts/`: Barlow Condensed and Share Tech Mono (OFL), from the owner's Potato Kicker

## Ground rules

- No cloud, no subscription, no logins. Everything runs locally inside the plugin.
- Tone comes from a real DSP engine. Since 2026-10-28 the owner allows ML anywhere it helps (e.g. Basic Pitch transcription, learned starting points for Match), and GPL code in the plugin, as it is a personal tool (GPL code means the repo and any build shared from it fall under the GPL).
- Audio thread is real-time safe: no allocation, locks, file I/O or model inference in `processBlock`/`renderNextBlock`.
- Training data: the owner's sounds, audio rendered from this engine, and other sources whose licence permits it. Check terms before use; Splice, Loopmasters and NI licences forbid AI training, personal use included.
- Git: commit locally; push to `github.com/basspotato/zero-engine` only when the owner asks.
- GPL code (Matchering, DawDreamer, Surge, Vital) is allowed (see above); note where it is used. Model and dataset licences still apply to training data.
